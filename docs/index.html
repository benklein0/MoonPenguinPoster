Our Story

To Give
or to Keep.

The Moon Penguin Shop was born from a love of beautiful, functional objects, the kind you reach for every day and feel a little spark of joy each time.

Every piece is made entirely by hand in Westport, Connecticut. Brass and steel charms are carefully set into richly colored resin, creating unique and useful accessories that are as beautiful as they are practical.

From pill boxes to compact mirrors, bag hooks to keychains, each item is a tiny work of art, made with intention and love.

The Process

Made entirely by hand,
every single time

01
Source & Select

Each charm is carefully chosen for its character and detail: celestial, botanical, nautical, or just plain cute!

02
Pour & Cure

Richly pigmented resin is carefully poured and cured, locking in color and creating a flawless, glossy surface around each charm.

03
Set & Polish

Each piece is set and polished by hand. The natural variation in resin means no two are exactly alike.

04
Pack with Love

Every order is carefully packaged and shipped from Westport, CT with care and attention.
